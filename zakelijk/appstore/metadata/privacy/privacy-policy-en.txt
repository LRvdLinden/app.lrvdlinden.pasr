Privacy in Pasr Zakelijk

Pasr Zakelijk is the app for businesses that use Pasr's digital stamp cards. The full privacy policy is at https://pasr.nl/privacy/#stempelkaarten-van-ondernemers.

What we store
- Your Pasr account: email address, first name and an account ID. You can delete your account in the app (Settings > Account).
- Your business: name, city, website, optional description and address (with its map position, looked up via Apple Maps).
- Your stamp cards: name, colour, the logo you choose, number of stamps and reward.
- Staff: the name and email address you enter when inviting someone.
- The log: every stamp, redemption and undo, with who did it and the type of device (iPhone or iPad).
- Customers: only their anonymous code and their progress. You see customers as the last characters of that code, never a name or email address.

What we don't do
- No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics or crash reporting.
- The camera reads QR codes on your device only; images are not stored or sent.
- Face ID happens entirely on your device; we never see any of it.

Where
Data is sent over HTTPS to the Pasr server (backend: Supabase) and kept until you delete the card, the business or your account. A business can't erase the log itself.

Questions: https://pasr.nl/support/
