Pasr is the free app for all your loyalty cards, gift cards and vouchers from Dutch stores. No more stack of plastic in your wallet: your cards live in one app, in the style of Apple Wallet. No ads, no subscription and nobody looking over your shoulder. The app is available in Dutch.

ADD CARDS IN SECONDS
• Scan your card with the camera: Pasr reads the barcode or QR code and recognises the store.
• Choose from more than 300 Dutch stores with the right logo and colour, or make your own card.
• Missing a store or gift card? Request it in the app; it is added without an update and you get a notification.

SWITCH IN ONE MINUTE
• Record your screen while you go through the cards in your old app. Pasr finds them all.
• Or pick screenshots, up to 50 at once. Everything is read on your own iPhone.

AT THE CHECKOUT
• One tap brings your card forward with the code at full brightness.
• All common codes: EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 128, Code 39, ITF, QR, PDF417 and Aztec.
• Alphabetical, with search, favourites and a filter by card type.

SHARE TOGETHER OR FOR A WHILE
• Shared Pasr: share cards with your family or housemates, up to 6 people, each with their own account.
• Temporary sharing: lend a card via WhatsApp or email until an end date. After that the code disappears. Extend or stop early at any time.
• Or share a card for good with a link or QR code.

APPLE WALLET AND NOTIFICATIONS
• Add a card to Apple Wallet. iOS shows it on your Lock Screen when you are at that store.
• Or let Pasr show your card as soon as you walk into a store, even when the app is closed. Pin your usual branch yourself.
• A reminder before a voucher or gift card expires.

GIFT CARDS AND VOUCHERS
• Keep track of balance, PIN and expiry date. After showing a card, Pasr asks what you spent, so the balance goes down.
• History per card: when and where you used it, like in Apple Wallet.

IPHONE, IPAD, APPLE WATCH AND MAC
• Your cards on your Apple Watch.
• Home Screen widgets with your most used cards.
• Also runs on Macs with Apple silicon and on Apple Vision Pro.
• Light, dark or automatic.
• Syncs through your own iCloud or a free Pasr account.

PRIVATE AND SECURE
• No ads, no tracking and no selling of data.
• Lock the app or single cards with Face ID.
• Block screenshots of your codes; temporarily shared cards are always protected.
• Optional free Pasr account with your cards stored encrypted (AES-256).
• Back-ups encrypted with your own password.

Made in the Netherlands, for Dutch stores. Questions? Email support@pasr.nl or visit pasr.nl.

Pasr is not affiliated with the stores in the app. Brand names and logos belong to their owners and are only used to recognise your own cards.
