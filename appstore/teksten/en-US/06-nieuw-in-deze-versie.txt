• Tickets from businesses: sign up for free through a business's link or QR code. Your ticket lives in Pasr in Apple Wallet style, with logo, date, time, doors, location with Directions and a large QR code. Tickets for the same event sit together; swipe to the next ticket.
• You get a notification the day before, when something changes and when you move up from the waiting list.
• A business's loyalty card: with barcode, the current or next discount, your savings and a notification for new discounts.
• Add to Pasr: tap the button on a business's website or open a pasr.nl/add link, and Pasr shows what you are adding.
• Tickets and loyalty cards from businesses can also go into Apple Wallet.
