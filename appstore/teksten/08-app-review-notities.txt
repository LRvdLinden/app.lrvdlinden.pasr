Pasr is a free wallet app for loyalty cards, gift cards and vouchers for Dutch stores. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. The app is in Dutch.

How to test:
1. Open the app and go through the welcome screens. An account is optional; tap "Ga verder zonder account" to continue without one.
2. Tap + > "Kaart toevoegen", choose a store (for example "Albert Heijn"), enter a number (for example 2620123456782) and save. Or tap "Scan je kaart" to scan any barcode.
3. Tap a card: it comes forward and shows the barcode at full screen brightness. "Voeg toe aan Apple Wallet" creates a signed Wallet pass via our server; nothing is stored there.
4. Import: + > "Importeren uit andere app" > choose a screen recording (or screenshots) that shows barcodes. Everything is read on the device.
5. Filter: the filter button next to + filters by card type (loyalty, gift cards, favourites, shared).
6. Temporary sharing: tap a card > share > "Tijdelijk", choose a period and share. The receiver adds the card in Pasr; after the end date the code is hidden and the link stops working. Settings > "Tijdelijk gedeeld" lets you extend, shorten, expire or stop it.
7. Shared Pasr (needs the demo account): Settings > "Gedeelde Pasr" > create a group and invite an email address. Members see the group's cards; when adding or editing a card, "Bewaren in" chooses private or the group.
8. Request a store (needs the demo account): + > "Kaart toevoegen" > "Mis je een winkel?" at the bottom. Added stores appear in the list without an app update, and the requester gets a push notification.

Demo account (sign-in fields in App Store Connect): already contains three cards and a shared Pasr "Gezin" with two cards, so Shared Pasr can be checked right away. Sign in via Settings > Account > "Inloggen of account maken".

Permissions:
- Camera: scanning barcodes (asked when scanning).
- Photos: only the photos or screen recording the user picks for import or a logo.
- Location (When In Use): only when the user turns on "Kaart tonen bij de winkel", sorts by "Dichtstbij eerst", taps "Ik sta hier" or picks Wallet branches. Used to find nearby branches in Apple Maps and to set local location notifications (UNLocationNotificationTrigger).
- Location (Always, optional): Settings > Meldingen > "Ook als Pasr dicht is". With it, significant-location-change monitoring wakes Pasr after the user has moved about a kilometre, so it can replace the 20 monitored stores with the ones around the new position (iOS allows 20 per app). No continuous tracking, no background location mode. The location never leaves the device.
- Notifications: local reminders, and push notifications only for "your requested store was added" and (opt-in) "new stores".
- Face ID: optional lock for the app or single cards.

Screenshot protection: codes of temporarily shared cards (and, if the user enables it, all codes) appear blank in screenshots and screen recordings, so a shared card really stops working after its end date.

The app also runs as an iPad app on Mac and Apple Vision Pro; camera scanning is hidden there.
A review prompt is shown at most once a week after the first hours of use. Settings contains a card "Meer apps van de ontwikkelaar" linking to the developer's website.
Store logos are shown only to help users recognise their own cards. Pasr is not affiliated with these stores.
